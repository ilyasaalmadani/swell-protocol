# Code of conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, by reference:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions, email and any other project space.

## Summary

- Be respectful and welcoming. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Accept fair criticism of your work.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Act in the interest of the project and its users.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to hello@jijilabsdigital.com. Reports are kept private. Maintainers who are not involved review each report and act according to the Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban). See also `GOVERNANCE.md`.
