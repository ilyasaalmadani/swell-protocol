# Security policy

The Swell Protocol has had internal reviews and formal models, and has not been independently audited yet. An independent audit is planned.

Report a vulnerability privately at https://github.com/swellchat/swell-protocol/security/advisories/new, or by email to hello@jijilabsdigital.com with the subject `Swell Protocol security`. Please do not open a public issue. Scope, safe harbor and Hall of Fame: https://getswell.org/security

We aim to reply within 7 days and to publish a fix and an advisory within 90 days. Reporters are credited unless they ask not to be.
